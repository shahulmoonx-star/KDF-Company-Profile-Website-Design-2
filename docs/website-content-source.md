### Who we are

> KDF sees itself as "The Best of its Class Company". ## Consolidated List of Inconsistencies Found on the Live Site

These are all things the current kdf.com.kw site itself is inconsistent about — worth resolving with the client before finalizing new-site copy, rather than something introduced in this extraction:

1. **Employee count:** "500+" (About page body) vs. "450" (Home page stat tile).
2. **ISO certification years:** 9001:2015 / 14001:2015 / 45001:2018 (OHS&E page) vs. a "2023" edition implied by the Certifications page's award context (Sixth Edition Amir's Award is dated 2023, and ASSP awards are 2024 — but the ISO years themselves are only stated once, on the OHS&E page, as the 2015/2015/2018 revisions).
3. **Company name punctuation:** "Kuwait Drilling Fluids & Oil Services" (most pages) vs. "Kuwait Drilling Fluids and Oil Services" (contact block on the Technical Query page) vs. "Kuwait Drilling Fluids & Oil Services S.A.K.C" (Quality Policy page) vs. the name given at this project's kickoff, "Kuwait Drilling Fluids & Oil Service **Company**."
4. **Footer copyright year:** "© 2022" on most pages, "© 2021" on Quality Objectives and OHS&E — indicating the footer is manually set per page, not dynamic.
5. **CEO's name:** never printed anywhere on the site, despite a full signed-in-spirit "Message from the CEO."
6. **Chairman's title:** "Abdulaziz Alaradi" is signed with no title text on the page itself (only inferred from the nav label "Chairman's Message").
7. **Quality Objectives page:** the ninth objective is cut off mid-sentence on the live site ("...resources for" — no continuation).
8. **"MISwaco" reference:** the OHS&E page names "KDFOS/MISwaco QOHSE Management System" — MI-SWACO is a Schlumberger drilling-fluids brand; unclear if this is current or a leftover from an old JV structure. Ask the client whether this should appear on the new site at all.
9. **Typos present in source copy** (reproduce with correction, don't carry the typo forward): "Amir's of Kuwait Award" → likely meant "Amir of Kuwait's Award"; "Lost Criculation" → "Lost Circulation"; "Reservior" → "Reservoir"; "Corrosion Inhibtor" / "Scale Inhibtor" → "Inhibitor"; "we does business" → "we do business"; trailing stray comma after "...Oil & Gas industry in Kuwait,.".
